Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect: Name, Email, Mobile number, Delivery address, Location (latitude/longitude) when you place an order or sign in with Google.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to process and deliver your orders, send order confirmations via WhatsApp, and improve your shopping experience.

3. Google Login

We use Google Sign-In for authentication. We only access your name, email and profile picture. We do not store your Google password.

4. Location Data

Location is collected only when you set a delivery address. It is stored in our database to help deliver your order accurately.

5. WhatsApp Notifications

When you place an order, your order details are sent to our shopkeeper via WhatsApp for processing.

6. Data Security

Your data is stored securely on MongoDB Atlas (cloud database) with encryption.

7. No Third-Party Selling

We never sell your personal data to any third party.

8. Delete Your Data

To delete your account and data, contact us at: psmegamartmart@gmail.com or WhatsApp: 9784721900

9. Refund Policy

If you receive a wrong or damaged product, contact us within 24 hours. We will replace or refund the product. Cash orders refunded via UPI.
